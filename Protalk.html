How it works

One simple job: turn a ringing phone into a booked appointment.

The flow is short on purpose. If a step doesn't help a homeowner get a time on your calendar, or keep someone safe, it isn't in there.

  1. Ring

    Answered on the first ring

    Overflow while your CSR is on another line, lunch breaks, after 5, weekends. You choose when Protalk takes the call and when it rings through to you first.

  2. First 5 seconds

    Honest greeting

    It greets the caller with your shop's name and says it's an AI and the call is recorded. California requires everyone's consent to record, so this line is never optional.

  3. Emergency screen

    Danger goes to a person

    A gas smell, a CO alarm, or sparking at the unit means it stops booking and transfers to your on-call tech. If nobody picks up, it tells the caller to step outside and call 911 or SDG&E, then texts you.

  4. Intake

    The details your dispatcher asks for

    Name, callback number, service address, the problem in the caller's own words, system age if they know it, and any pets or gate codes.

  5. Service area

    Checks it's your territory

    Uses your ZIP code list. Out-of-area callers get a polite no and a referral if you want to give one.

  6. Scheduling

    Offers windows you actually have

    Reads your calendar live and offers two choices. It never promises an arrival time or a price you haven't approved.

  7. Booked

    On your calendar, in your inbox

    The appointment lands with full notes, the caller gets a confirmation, and you get a summary text and the recording.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Is it a real person?

No. It's an AI voice assistant, and it says so at the start of every call. We think customers deserve to know, and most of them just care that someone picked up and got them on the schedule.

What happens with an emergency like a gas smell?

It stops trying to book. It tells the caller to get to safety and transfers them to your on-call number, then texts you a summary. You decide what counts as an emergency for your shop.

Will it replace my office person?

That's not the goal. Most shops start with overflow and after-hours calls, the ones going to voicemail today. Your office keeps the relationships. Protalk catches what falls through.

Are the calls recorded? Is that legal in California?

Calls are recorded, and every caller hears that at the very start. California requires all parties to consent to recording, so the disclosure is built in and can't be turned off. This isn't legal advice; we're glad to walk your attorney through how it works.

Does it work with my scheduling software?

Today it books into Google Calendar. Jobber and Housecall Pro are next on our list. If you run something else, tell us during the consult.

Can I change what it says?

Yes. You approve the script before it goes live, and you can text us changes anytime, like a new service area, holiday hours, or a seasonal tune-up special.

What does it cost?

We're setting pricing with our first North County shops right now. Book a consult and we'll walk through what makes sense for the size of your shop.

Where are you located?

San Marcos, California. We work with shops across North County San Diego and can meet you at your shop.
